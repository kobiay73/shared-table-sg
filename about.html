About the journal

Food travels.
We follow.

Our point of view

The Shared Table is a Singapore-based journal about the foods of the Mediterranean, Middle East and Balkans. We tell the histories behind dishes, meet the people keeping traditions alive and notice how recipes change when they reach a new table.

We avoid reducing a wide region to a single style. Specific places, communities and sources matter.

Editorial standards

We distinguish reported stories, guides and opinion. Restaurant invitations and commercial relationships do not guarantee positive coverage and are disclosed when relevant.

We welcome corrections and additional cultural context at hello@sharedtable.sg.
